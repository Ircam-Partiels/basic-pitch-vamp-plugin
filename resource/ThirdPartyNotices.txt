Models
------

The model embedded in the Resources/models folder of the plugin bundle
(nmp.tflite) is the model of Basic Pitch (https://github.com/spotify/basic-pitch)
developed by Spotify's Audio Intelligence Lab. It is distributed without
modification under the Apache License 2.0 (Apache-2.0.txt). The pre-processing
of the audio, the post-processing of the outputs of the model and the creation
of the notes of the plugin are ported from the Basic Pitch Python library
(basic_pitch.inference and basic_pitch.note_creation), also distributed under
the Apache License 2.0.

- nmp.tflite (ICASSP 2022 model, Basic Pitch v0.4.0)
  https://github.com/spotify/basic-pitch/tree/v0.4.0/basic_pitch/saved_models/icassp_2022
  Copyright 2022 Spotify AB
  License: Apache License 2.0 (Apache-2.0.txt)
